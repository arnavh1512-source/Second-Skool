# Second Skool — Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 1.0 (draft — not published)**
**Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

This policy forms part of the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md). It applies to every person who uses Second Skool — centre heads, teachers, administrators and students alike.

The short version: use it to run your coaching centre. Do not use it to harm anybody, and do not use it to break the platform.

---

## 1. Do not misuse other people's data

You must not:

- upload personal data you have no lawful basis to hold, or for which you have not obtained the consent the law requires,
- upload personal data about people who have nothing to do with your Centre,
- upload the categories the Terms exclude — health records, biometrics, caste, religion, financial account numbers, or government identifiers such as Aadhaar or PAN,
- extract data about students or staff for any purpose other than running your Centre,
- share, sell or transfer student or guardian contact details to anyone outside your Centre,
- use guardian contact details for marketing unrelated to the student's education, or
- retain records for a student who has left when you no longer have a reason to.

**Anything involving a child's data is treated as high severity.** See the [Children's Data Addendum](./childrens-data-addendum.md).

---

## 2. Do not misuse the account system

You must not:

- share a login, or let someone use an account that is not theirs,
- approve a person into your Centre who has no legitimate reason to be there,
- share join codes publicly or with people outside your Centre,
- impersonate another person, centre or organisation,
- create an account using false information, or
- attempt to access a centre, record or account you have not been granted access to.

---

## 3. Do not attack or degrade the platform

You must not:

- probe, scan or penetration-test the service without our prior written consent,
- attempt to bypass authentication, authorisation, rate limits or tenant isolation,
- attempt to access another Centre's data by any means,
- reverse-engineer, decompile or disassemble the software,
- introduce malware, or upload a file intended to exploit a vulnerability,
- scrape, crawl or use automated means to extract data at a rate that degrades service for others,
- interfere with, overload or disrupt the service or its infrastructure, or
- resell or resupply the service to a third party.

**Responsible disclosure is welcome.** If you find a vulnerability, report it privately to [SECURITY EMAIL] and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it. We will not pursue action against anyone who reports in good faith, tests only against their own centre, and does not access, alter or exfiltrate other people's data.

---

## 4. Do not publish harmful content

Notes, assignments, announcements, messages and any other content you put into the platform must not:

- be unlawful under Indian law,
- harass, bully, threaten or intimidate any person — **including a student**,
- be obscene, sexually explicit, or in any way sexualise a minor,
- promote violence, self-harm, terrorism or the use of illegal substances,
- promote hatred or discrimination on grounds of religion, caste, race, sex, disability, place of birth or any similar ground,
- infringe someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights,
- be knowingly false, or be deployed to deceive,
- be spam or bulk unsolicited messaging, or
- impersonate another person.

Notifications sent through the platform must be operational — class schedules, fees, results, announcements about your Centre. Do not use them for promotional messaging to students or guardians.

---

## 5. Do not use it for what it is not

Second Skool is a tuition-management tool for education providers. It is not a general messaging service, a customer database, a marketing platform, a payment gateway for your own fee collection, or a file-sharing service. Do not use it as one.

---

## 6. Enforcement

Where we become aware of a breach, we may — proportionately to its severity and to the risk to others:

1. contact you and ask you to fix it,
2. remove or disable the specific content or access,
3. suspend the individual user account,
4. suspend the Centre, or
5. terminate the Centre and, where the law requires, report the matter to the authorities.

For breaches involving children's safety, unlawful content, or an attack on the platform, we may act immediately and without prior notice.

We will restore access once a breach is remedied, unless the breach was serious enough that we do not consider continued service appropriate.

Suspension or termination for breach of this policy does not entitle you to a refund.
