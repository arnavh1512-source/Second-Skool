# Children's Data Addendum

**Version 1.0 (draft — not published)**
**Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

This Addendum supplements the [Data Processing Agreement](./data-processing-agreement.md) between [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Processor", "we") and the coaching centre ("Fiduciary", "you"). It applies whenever personal data of a Child is processed through Second Skool — which, for a tuition-management platform, is nearly always.

Where this Addendum conflicts with the DPA or the Terms of Service, this Addendum prevails.

---

## 1. Why this document exists separately

Section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 places obligations on a Data Fiduciary handling children's data that go beyond the ordinary regime, and attaches the Act's heaviest penalties to failures involving children. A coaching centre is, by definition, processing children's data at scale.

We have pulled these obligations into their own document so that neither party can claim they were buried in a schedule.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Child** — an individual who has not completed eighteen years of age.
- **Parent** — a parent or lawful guardian of a Child.
- **Person with Disability** — an individual with a disability who has a lawful guardian appointed under applicable law. Under the DPDP Act such an individual's data attracts protections equivalent to a Child's.
- **Verifiable Consent** — consent of a Parent obtained by a means that gives reasonable assurance that the person giving it is in fact the Child's parent or lawful guardian and is an identifiable adult.

Other capitalised terms have the meaning given in the DPA.

---

## 3. Your obligations — verifiable parental consent

3.1 **Before entering a Child's personal data into the Platform**, you must obtain Verifiable Consent from that Child's Parent.

3.2 The consent must be:

- **free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous**, given by a clear affirmative action,
- **for a stated purpose**, limited to the personal data necessary for that purpose,
- **capable of being withdrawn** as easily as it was given, and
- **recorded**, with the date, the identity of the person who gave it, the purposes consented to, and the means by which the parental relationship was verified.

3.3 **Bundling is not consent.** Consent buried in an enrolment form's fine print, or made a precondition of enrolment for purposes that are not necessary for enrolment, is not valid consent under the Act.

3.4 **You will retain consent records** for as long as you process the Child's data and for [CONSENT RECORD RETENTION YEARS] years afterwards, and will produce them to us, to a Parent, or to the Data Protection Board on request.

3.5 **You will re-obtain consent** where the purpose of processing materially changes.

3.6 **On withdrawal of consent**, you will stop processing that Child's data for the withdrawn purpose without undue delay, and delete it unless a law requires you to keep it.

> **What counts as verifiable in practice.** The Act does not prescribe a single method. Reasonable approaches for a coaching centre include: a signed physical consent form collected at enrolment and retained; a consent captured against a Parent's verified mobile number by OTP; or consent given in person before staff who record it. A tick-box completed by an unidentified person online, on its own, is unlikely to be sufficient. Take your own advice on the method you adopt.

## 4. Prohibited processing — both parties

Neither party will, in relation to a Child's personal data:

4.1 **Track or monitor behaviour.** No behavioural tracking, no profiling, no inference of characteristics, preferences or performance predictions beyond the assessment records the Centre itself enters.

4.2 **Target advertising.** No advertising directed at a Child, and no use of a Child's data for advertising to anyone.

4.3 **Undertake processing likely to cause detrimental effect on the wellbeing of a Child.**

4.4 **Sell, rent, license or otherwise make available** a Child's personal data to any third party other than a Sub-processor listed under the DPA.

4.5 **Use a Child's data to train machine-learning models.**

We confirm that the Platform contains no advertising technology, no third-party analytics or tracking pixels, and no profiling engine, and that we will not introduce any without first amending this Addendum on notice to you.

---

## 5. Data minimisation for Children

5.1 You will enter only the personal data about a Child that is necessary for the Centre's stated educational purposes.

5.2 You will **not** enter into the Platform, in relation to any Child:

- health or medical records,
- biometric data, including photographs used for identification,
- caste, religion, or community,
- government identity numbers, including Aadhaar and PAN,
- financial account or card details,
- home address, unless you have a specific documented need,
- data about the Child's family beyond the guardian contact necessary for communication.

5.3 The Platform is not designed to hold the categories in 5.2, and holds no additional safeguards for them. If you need to record any of them, do so outside the Platform or contact us before doing so.

---

## 6. Parents' rights

6.1 A Parent may exercise, on behalf of their Child, every right the DPDP Act gives a Data Principal — access to a summary of the data held, correction, completion, updating, erasure, and withdrawal of consent.

6.2 **Requests go to you, not to us.** You are the Data Fiduciary. If a Parent contacts us, we will forward the request to you within 48 hours, tell the Parent we have done so, and not respond substantively ourselves.

6.3 You will acknowledge a Parent's request within 48 hours and respond substantively within 30 days, or such shorter period as the law requires.

6.4 You will publish, at your Centre and in your own privacy notice, the contact point at which a Parent can make such a request.

6.5 We will provide reasonable technical assistance to help you fulfil a request, as set out in clause 8 of the DPA.

## 7. When a Child turns 18

7.1 A Student who reaches eighteen becomes a Data Principal in their own right. Parental consent no longer covers them.

7.2 You are responsible for identifying Students who reach eighteen and, where you continue to process their data, obtaining their own consent.

7.3 The Platform does not currently do this for you. [PRODUCT NOTE: a date-of-birth field and an age-transition prompt would make this manageable — worth building before scale.]

---

## 8. Persons with disability

Where a Student is a person with disability having a lawful guardian, every obligation in this Addendum applies with the guardian in place of the Parent, and the guardian's authority must be verified against the appointing order or certificate.

---

## 9. Breaches involving Children's data

9.1 We will treat any Personal Data Breach affecting a Child's data as high severity, regardless of record count.

9.2 We will notify you within **24 hours** of becoming aware of such a breach — shorter than the 48 hours in the DPA — because your own notification clock to Parents and to the Data Protection Board starts at that point.

9.3 We will support you in notifying Parents, including by identifying the specific Children and data categories affected.

## 11. Consequences

You acknowledge that a failure to obtain Verifiable Consent is a failure by you as Data Fiduciary, not by us as Processor, and that our provision of the Platform is expressly conditional on the warranty you give in section 4 of the Terms of Service and clause 3 of the DPA.

We may suspend processing for a Centre where we have a reasonable belief, on specific evidence, that Children's data is being processed without a lawful basis, after giving you notice and a reasonable opportunity to respond — except where the risk of harm requires immediate action.

---

## 12. Product commitments we make to you

For as long as this Addendum is in force:

- the Platform will contain no advertising, no third-party behavioural analytics and no tracking pixels;
- no Child's data will be used to train a model;
- no Child will be profiled by us;
- data collected about a Child will be limited to what a Centre enters through the Platform's own fields;
- notifications sent to a Child's device will be operational (class, fee, result, announcement) and never promotional.

If any of these ceases to be true, we will notify you at least 30 days before the change and you may terminate without penalty.
