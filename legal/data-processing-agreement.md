## 1. Definitions

Terms defined in the DPDP Act have the same meaning here. In particular:

- **Personal Data** — any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to it.
- **Data Principal** — the individual the personal data relates to; for a child, includes the parent or lawful guardian.
- **Data Fiduciary** — the person who determines the purpose and means of processing.
- **Data Processor** — a person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
- **Personal Data Breach** — any unauthorised processing, or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, use, alteration, destruction or loss of access to personal data, that compromises its confidentiality, integrity or availability.
- **Centre Data** — personal data processed by us on your behalf through the Platform, as described in Schedule A.
- **Child** — an individual under the age of eighteen years.
- **Sub-processor** — a third party engaged by us to process Centre Data.

## 3. Your obligations as Data Fiduciary

You warrant and undertake that:

3.1 **Lawful basis.** You have a lawful basis under the DPDP Act for every item of Centre Data you cause to be processed.

3.2 **Notice.** You have given every Data Principal the notice section 5 of the DPDP Act requires, in the form and languages it requires.

3.3 **Verifiable parental consent.** For every Student who is a Child, and for every Data Principal who is a person with disability having a lawful guardian, you have obtained verifiable consent from the parent or lawful guardian before entering their data, and you retain evidence of it.

3.4 **Accuracy.** You are responsible for the accuracy and currency of Centre Data. We do not verify it.

3.5 **Data minimisation.** You will not enter personal data beyond what your stated purpose requires, and in particular will not enter the categories excluded by clause 4.4.

3.6 **Access control.** You will grant Platform access only to people who need it, and will revoke it promptly when they no longer do.

3.7 **Data Principal requests.** You will handle requests from Data Principals. We will assist you as set out in clause 8.

3.8 **Your own notifications.** Where a Personal Data Breach triggers a notification duty that falls on you as Data Fiduciary, you will make that notification. We will give you what you need to do it.

You indemnify us against loss arising from breach of this clause 3, as provided in the Terms of Service.

4.8 **Assistance.** We will give you reasonable assistance, at your cost where the effort is more than trivial, with data protection impact assessments, consultations with the Data Protection Board, and security reviews.

## SCHEDULE B — Technical and organisational measures

**Tenant isolation**
- Every record carries the identifier of the Centre it belongs to.
- Database row-level security enforces isolation at the storage engine, keyed to the authenticated user's Centre. It is not an application-layer check that a bug could bypass.
- Columns that determine a user's role, staff status and Centre membership are revoked from ordinary application credentials and can only be written by audited privileged server routes.

**Access control**
- Authentication via Supabase Auth: email/password or Google OAuth.
- Role-based access within a Centre — head, teacher, student — enforced in the database, not only in the interface.
- Administrative access to the platform-wide operator console requires an allow-listed identity, a confirmed email address **and** a verified Google identity; all three must hold.
- Privileged server credentials exist only in server-side environment variables and are never sent to a browser.

**Encryption**
- All traffic encrypted in transit with TLS.
- Data encrypted at rest by the database provider.

**Resilience**
- Automated database backups by the hosting provider. [CONFIRM: point-in-time recovery enabled? If not, enable it before signing a customer.]
- Infrastructure operated on managed platforms with provider-level redundancy.

**Monitoring and abuse prevention**
- Rate limiting on API endpoints, per authenticated identity.
- Structured server-side logging of security-relevant events, including privileged access and rejected authorisation attempts.
- Error monitoring, where enabled, configured not to capture personal data payloads.

**Software assurance**
- Type-checked codebase; automated test suite run before every release.
- Input validated at every API boundary.
- Dependencies from public registries, with security advisories monitored.

**Organisational**
- Personnel bound by written confidentiality obligations.
- Access on a need-to-know basis; access removed on departure.
- Documented breach-response procedure.
