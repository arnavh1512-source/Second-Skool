If something about how your personal data is handled is wrong, this page tells you exactly who to write to and what will happen.

Two Indian laws require us to publish this: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (right to grievance redressal) and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021 (Grievance Officer).

---

## First — check who holds your data

**If you are a student or a parent**, your records were entered by a **coaching centre**, not by us. The centre is the Data Fiduciary. It decides what is recorded and it is the one that must answer you.

👉 **Contact your coaching centre first.** They can correct or delete your records directly.

If the centre does not respond within a reasonable time, or refuses without explanation, write to us. We cannot release or change their records for you, but we will forward your request to them within 48 hours, tell you we have done so, and require them to respond under our agreement with them.

**If you hold a Second Skool account in your own name** — a centre owner, or anyone we deal with directly — write to us. We are the Data Fiduciary for that data.

## What to include

To help us act quickly, tell us:

1. **Your name and how to reach you.**
2. **Which coaching centre** the data relates to, if any.
3. **What the complaint is** — inaccurate data, data held without consent, a refusal to delete, a suspected breach, or something else.
4. **What you want us to do.**
5. **Anything supporting it** — a screenshot, an email, dates.

If you are a parent writing about your child, say so and give the child's name as recorded at the centre.

## Escalating

### To the Data Protection Board of India

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your complaint, you may take it to the **Data Protection Board of India**, established under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

You must normally have raised the complaint with the Data Fiduciary first and either received an unsatisfactory response or received none within the required period.

Current filing details and contact information for the Board are published by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology at **[meity.gov.in](https://www.meity.gov.in)**. We will provide the current details on request.

### To a consumer forum

If the complaint concerns a service or billing failure rather than data protection, you may also approach a District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

## A note on good faith

We will treat every complaint seriously, and we will not retaliate against anyone for making one.

The DPDP Act also places a duty on you: not to make a false or frivolous complaint, not to impersonate another person, and not to suppress material information when exercising your rights. Penalties can apply.
