# Second Skool — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 (draft — not published)**
**Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]** · **Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

This policy explains what personal data Second Skool handles, why, and what you can do about it. It doubles as the notice required under section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 for the data we handle in our own right.

We have tried to write it in plain language. Where a sentence has legal weight we have said so rather than hiding it.

---

## 1. Two different roles — find yours first

Second Skool is a tool that coaching centres use to run their classes. That means we sit in two different positions depending on whose data is involved.

**If you are a student or a parent** — your data was entered by your coaching centre. **The coaching centre is the Data Fiduciary; we are only its Data Processor.** We hold the data on their behalf and act on their instructions. We cannot show you your records, change them or delete them on your say-so, because they are not ours to release. Ask your coaching centre. If they do not respond, you can still write to us (section 9) and we will route it to them and press them for an answer.

**If you signed your centre up** — for your own account details (your name, email, phone, billing records) **we are the Data Fiduciary** and this policy is our notice to you.

**If you are a teacher or staff member** — your profile exists because your centre added you. Your centre is the Fiduciary for your record within the centre; we are the Fiduciary for the sign-in account itself.

## 2. What we handle

### 2.1 Data you give us as an account holder

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email address | To create and secure your account, and to contact you |
| Phone number | Support, and account recovery |

### 2.2 Centre Data — held on behalf of a coaching centre

Entered by the centre, not by us. Typically:

- Student name, class or batch, roll or admission number, school
- Guardian name and contact number
- Attendance records
- Test and exam results
- Fee records — amounts due, paid, and dates
- Timetable and class assignments
- Notes, assignments and announcements the centre publishes
- Staff names, roles and approval status

Much of this is **data about children**. See section 6.

### 2.3 Data generated by using the service

| Data | Why | Kept for |

| Web push subscription (an endpoint issued by your browser vendor, plus keys) | To send you notifications, only if you turn them on | Until you turn notifications off or the browser revokes it |
| Server logs — request path, timestamp, coarse status, and a user identifier | Security, abuse prevention, debugging | [LOG RETENTION DAYS] days |

### 2.4 What we do not do

We do **not** use advertising cookies, analytics pixels, third-party trackers, cross-site tracking, or any behavioural advertising. We do not sell or rent personal data. We do not use Centre Data to train machine-learning models. We do not profile students.

The only browser storage we set is what is needed to keep you signed in and, if you enable them, to deliver notifications.

## 3. Why we process it, and on what basis

For **account holders** (our own users), we process personal data:

- **on your consent**, which you give by creating an account after reading this notice, to provide and secure the service and to contact you about it;
- for **legitimate uses** recognised by the DPDP Act, including responding to your support requests and complying with a legal obligation;
- to **comply with law**, including tax and accounting record-keeping.

For **Centre Data**, our basis is the instruction of the coaching centre under our [Data Processing Agreement](./data-processing-agreement.md). The lawful basis for collecting that data in the first place — including verifiable parental consent for children — is the centre's responsibility.

We do not use your data for any purpose incompatible with the above without asking you first.

## 4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers ("sub-processors"). Each is bound to confidentiality and to process data only for us.

The current list, with what each one handles and where, is maintained at **[sub-processors.md](./sub-processors.md)**. In summary: hosting and database (Supabase), application hosting (Vercel), sign-in and push delivery (Google), payments (Razorpay).

Beyond those, we disclose personal data only:

- when you tell us to,
- to a professional adviser under a duty of confidence,
- to a successor entity in a merger or sale of the business, on notice to you, or
- where we are legally compelled to — a valid court order, a lawful demand from a government agency, or a statutory obligation. Where we are permitted to tell you, we will.

**We do not share anything with data brokers, ad networks or analytics resellers.**

## 6. Children's data

Most students on this platform are under 18. This matters and we treat it as such.

- **We do not knowingly collect data directly from a child.** Children do not create accounts with us. A student's record is created by their coaching centre.
- **Verifiable parental consent is obtained by the coaching centre**, not by us, and the centre warrants to us in writing that it holds it.
- **We never track children behaviourally, profile them, or target advertising at them.** The DPDP Act prohibits this and we have no mechanism that could do it.
- **We collect the minimum.** We do not ask centres for children's photographs, biometrics, health data, caste, religion or government identity numbers, and our terms tell them not to upload such data.

If you are a parent and you believe a coaching centre has recorded your child's data without your consent, contact the centre first. You may also write to us at [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will notify the centre, require them to respond, and — if the centre does not resolve it — take the matter up with them under our agreement.

Full detail is in the [Children's Data Addendum](./childrens-data-addendum.md).

## 8. How we protect it

- Every centre's data is isolated at the database level by row-level security, so one centre's queries cannot reach another's rows.
- All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
- The privileged key that can bypass tenant isolation exists only on the server, never in the browser, and is used by a small number of audited server routes.
- Passwords are never stored by us in readable form; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, and Google sign-in is available.
- Administrative access to production is restricted, requires a verified Google identity, and is logged.
- API endpoints are rate limited.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will act on the plan in section 10.

## 9. Your rights

If we are the Data Fiduciary for your data (see section 1), you have the right to:

- **know** what personal data of yours we hold and who we have shared it with,
- **correct** data that is inaccurate, and **complete** or **update** it,
- **erase** data we no longer need for the purpose it was collected for or to meet a legal obligation,
- **withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it — note this may mean we can no longer provide the service,
- **nominate** another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated, and
- **complain** — see below.

To exercise any of these, write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] with enough detail for us to identify your account. We will acknowledge within **48 hours** and respond substantively within **30 days**. We may need to verify who you are first; we will not ask for more identification than necessary.

**If your data was entered by a coaching centre**, we will forward your request to them within 48 hours and tell you we have done so. We cannot act on it directly.

You also have a duty under the DPDP Act not to make a false or frivolous complaint and not to impersonate another person when exercising these rights.

## 10. If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach occurs, we will:

1. contain and investigate it immediately,
2. notify the affected coaching centre without undue delay, with what we know,
3. notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals to the extent the law requires of us in our role, and support each centre in meeting the notification duties that fall on them as Data Fiduciary,
4. tell you what happened, what data was involved, what we have done, and what you should do.

We will not stay quiet to protect our reputation.
