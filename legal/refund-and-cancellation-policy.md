## 3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your account settings, or by emailing [BILLING EMAIL].

- Cancellation stops the **next** renewal. It is not a refund of the current period.
- You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
- After that, your centre becomes read-only for 30 days so you can export your data, and is then deleted.
- **Deleting your centre from within the app is different and immediate.** It removes all student, staff, attendance, fee and result records at once and cannot be undone. Cancel your subscription if you want the 30-day export window; delete the centre only when you are sure.

## 4. Refunds

### 4.1 First subscription — 7-day money back

If this is your Centre's first paid subscription and you are not satisfied, email [BILLING EMAIL] within **7 days** of the first charge and we will refund it in full. No reason required.

### 4.2 Renewals

Renewal charges are **not refundable** as a matter of course. You have had notice of the renewal date and the ability to cancel before it.

We will make an exception, at our discretion, where:

- **you were charged in error** — a duplicate charge, a charge after cancellation, or the wrong amount. Refunded in full, always.
- **you renewed and did not use the service at all in the new period**, and you contact us within 14 days of the charge. We will normally refund in full.
- **we had a prolonged outage** that materially prevented you from using the service. We will refund pro rata for the affected days.
- **we terminate the service** or discontinue the platform. We refund the unused portion of any prepaid period pro rata.
- **we materially change the Terms and you reject the change** before it takes effect. We refund the unused portion pro rata.

### 4.3 Annual plans

Cancelled mid-term, an annual plan is not automatically refunded. Where you have a genuine reason — your centre has closed, for instance — email [BILLING EMAIL] and we will consider a pro-rata refund of the whole unused months, less any discount you received for paying annually. We would rather deal with this reasonably than argue.

### 4.4 No refund where

- your access was suspended or terminated for breach of the [Terms](./terms-of-service.md) or the [Acceptable Use Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md),
- you deleted your own centre and lost your data,
- you simply stopped using the service without cancelling, beyond the window in 4.2, or
- the request relates to fees your centre collected from its own students.

## 5. How refunds are paid

- Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method**. We cannot pay to a different account.
- We will approve or decline within **5 business days** of your request, and tell you which.
- Once approved, we initiate the refund within **[REFUND INITIATION DAYS] business days**.
- Your bank or card issuer then takes its own time — typically **5–10 business days**, sometimes longer. That part is outside our control.
- Refunds are of the amount charged including GST. Payment-gateway charges are borne by us, not deducted from your refund.
